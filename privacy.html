Privacy

How Scripture Ripples treats your information.

No accounts and no data collection

Scripture Ripples does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect, sell, or send personal information to a server of this project.

What stays on your device

If you use Build Your Pond, the stones and verses you save stay in this browser’s local storage on your device. If the browser blocks that storage, the pond is kept only in memory until you leave the page. You can delete a stone or clear the pond from Build Your Pond.

A reading-plan checkmark and any comparison draft you choose to save also stay in this browser, on this device.

Scripture and references

The Bible text shown in the app is the public-domain King James Version (KJV).

Published cross-references come from OpenBible.info and are used under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY) license.
