Privacy

How Scripture Ripples treats your information.

No accounts and no data collection

Scripture Ripples does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect, sell, or send personal information to a server of this project. There is no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

If you use Build Your Pond, the stones and verses you save are stored only on this device. Reading-plan checkmarks from Bible in a Year and any comparison draft you choose to save are stored only on this device too. So is your Commonplace Book: the links you find in the guided pond, the seals you earn, and your sound setting. If the device won’t allow storage, they are kept only until you close Scripture Ripples.

In the iPhone and iPad app, this information is kept in the app’s own storage on the device. On the website, it is kept in your web browser’s storage on the device.

How to delete your data

  • Saved stones: delete a stone or clear the pond in Build Your Pond.
  • Reading plan: untick a day’s checkmark in Bible in a Year.
  • Commonplace Book: open it from the red ribbon on the home page and choose Clear the book.
  • Everything at once: in the app, delete Scripture Ripples from your device. On the website, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Optional commentary request

The Bible text, cross-references, and Matthew Henry commentary are built in. Only when you open the optional Keil and Delitzsch · In-depth commentary section does Scripture Ripples request that chapter’s commentary from bible.helloao.org, a free public-domain Bible service. Like any web request, that service receives your device’s IP address and the chapter requested. Scripture Ripples sends it no personal information.

Links to other sites

Links to outside sites, such as source references, open in Safari (in the app, in a Safari window over the app). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Scripture and references

The Bible text shown in the app is the public-domain King James Version (KJV).

Published cross-references come from OpenBible.info and are used under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY) license.
