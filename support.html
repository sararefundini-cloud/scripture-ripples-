Common questions

Where are my saved stones? Open Build Your Pond. Your stones, reading-plan checkmarks, and drafts are stored only on this device, so they don’t move to a new phone.

How do I open a stone? Tap Explore Full Pond, then tap a stone to read its KJV verses and connections.

What do TEXT, INFERENCE, and TYPOLOGY mean? TEXT is what the verse says. INFERENCE is a reading of it. TYPOLOGY is a traditional connection between passages.

Does it work offline? Yes. Everything is built in except the optional Keil and Delitzsch commentary, which needs an internet connection.

How do I delete my data? See the Privacy page.
